Privacy Policy

Last updated: [August 2026]

This page explains what information inspocult ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, and how it's used. inspocult is a free browser-based template editor — most of what you do happens locally in your own browser and is never sent to us.

The short version

We don't require an account, we don't ask for your photos or personal files (everything you upload stays in your browser and is never sent to a server), and we don't sell your data. This site shows ads through Google AdSense to stay free, and AdSense uses cookies to do that — details below, along with how to opt out.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use inspocult. The photos, videos, and text you add inside the editor are processed entirely in your own browser and are not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools (if any are enabled) may automatically log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, for security and basic usage statistics.

Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's Partner Sites policy.

Third-party links

This site may link out to third-party services (for example, if you choose to purchase a template add-on or contact us via email). We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties — please review their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

inspocult is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

Because this app runs locally in your browser, you can clear your browser's local storage and cookies at any time to remove any locally-stored preferences (like which grid layout you last used). Doing so won't affect anything else on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
