Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This page explains what information inspocult ("we", "us") collects, why, where it's kept, and the choices you have. inspocult has two parts: the template studio, which works without an account, and the content planner, which needs a free account so your plans are saved and you can open them on any device.

The short version

  • The studio needs no account. Photos, videos and designs you make there stay in your browser and are never uploaded to us.
  • The planner needs an account (email + password). We store your planned posts: dates, captions, hashtags, titles and small preview images (about 50 KB each). Your full-size photos and videos are not uploaded; they stay on the device where you made the post.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't use your planner content for advertising.
  • The site shows ads through Google AdSense to stay free. Google uses cookies for this, which you can control (details below).
  • You can ask us to see, download or delete your data at any time.

Who is responsible for your data

inspocult is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at shopinspocult@gmail.com for any privacy question or request.

What stays on your device

Some data is stored only in your own browser and never sent to us:

  • Everything you do in the studio (your photos, videos, text and designs) until you choose to export it.
  • The full-size images and videos of your planned posts, kept in your browser's storage on the computer or phone where you made the post, so you can download them at posting time.
  • A login token that keeps you signed in, and a few preferences.

You can remove these by logging out and ticking "Also remove my planner and full-size files from this computer", or by clearing your browser's site data. Planner backup files you download are saved on your device and are entirely under your control.

Where your data is kept, and who helps us

We use a small number of trusted service providers ("processors") who handle data on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase stores accounts and planner data (including preview images). Our database is hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files and may log technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Google (AdSense) serves ads; Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries the site needs. These services receive your IP address when your browser loads them.

Some of these providers are based in, or may process data in, countries outside the European Economic Area (for example the United States). When that happens, they rely on approved safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google's Ads Settings or at www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • More detail: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

We don't share your account email or planner content with Google or any advertiser.

How long we keep it

  • Account and planner data: for as long as you have an account. Posts you delete are removed from our database, together with their preview images, straight away.
  • When you delete your account: your account and all planner data are deleted immediately (or within 30 days if you ask us by email). Copies may remain in our provider's routine backups for a short time before being overwritten.
  • Technical logs: kept by our providers for a limited period for security, then deleted.

Your rights

Under data-protection law (including the GDPR) you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you, and get a copy (the planner's Backup button also lets you download your planner yourself);
  • correct data that's wrong;
  • delete your account and data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent (for example for ad cookies) at any time;
  • data portability: receive your data in a common, machine-readable format.

Deleting your account: you can do it yourself at any time. In the studio, click your initial (top right) → Delete account…. This immediately deletes your account, all your planner posts and their preview images.

To use any of the other rights, or if you can't log in, email shopinspocult@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data-protection authority. In Portugal that's the CNPD (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados).

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Our database uses access rules so that each account can only ever read or change its own planner data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

inspocult isn't intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to create a planner account (or older if the law in your country requires it). We don't knowingly collect data from children under that age; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Third-party links

This site may link to other websites and services. We aren't responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as inspocult changes or for legal reasons. We'll change the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes that affect account holders we'll let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email shopinspocult@gmail.com.
